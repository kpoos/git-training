## Hands-on labs

Use the matching lab in the [lab and facilitator guide](./labs/README.md):
For learner-ready task instructions and acceptance checks, see the
[hands-on task sheet](./hands-on-tasks.md).

1. Make and inspect a local change.
2. Integrate two branches.
3. Publish and synchronize team branches.
4. Review and improve a pull request.
5. Resolve a conflict and recover safely.
6. Ship a small contribution as a team.
